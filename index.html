About

I’m a PhD researcher at the University of Amsterdam’s GRAPPA institute, supervised by Dr. Samaya Nissanke, Dr. Christoph Weniger, and Dr. Philipp Mösta. Originally from Barpeta, Assam, India, my work bridges gravitational wave astrophysics, Bayesian statistics, and machine learning.

Research

My primary focus is on simulation-based inference for gravitational wave signals. I lead development of PEREGRINE, a scalable library for parameter estimation in gravitational wave astronomy. I collaborate with LIGO, Virgo, and KAGRA on detection and data analysis methods.

Selected Publications

  • Sequential simulation-based inference for gravitational wave signals
  • PEREGRINE: A simulation-based inference toolkit
  • Advanced Bayesian methods in GW astronomy
